      What this replaces
      
      
      Today each of these starts with a person. Deepgram’s pages give three different ways to ask for the SOC 2 report, and the healthcare agreement goes through a contact form.
      
      
      I have no number for how long that takes. Measuring it is week 1 of the plan.
      
    
      Choices I made in this page
      
      
        
        
      • The “no” answers are on the first screen.A reviewer who finds them later trusts nothing else.
        • 
        
      • One agreement unlocks every confidential report.No asking again, report by report.
        • 
        
      • The packet is the unit, not the document.A reviewer’s job is a checklist. The page keeps it for them.
        • 
        
      • Proposed answers are labeled as proposed.A trust center that overstates is worse than none.
