What changed for federal buyers in 2026

Three rule changes make the federal route shorter than it was, and one makes Deepgram’s FIPS work timely.

  • FedRAMP added an entry tier.A SOC 2 Type II report qualifies a vendor to submit for it. The older Moderate level is now called Class C. FedRAMP, July 30, 2026
  • The old process is closing.FedRAMP stops accepting new applications under it on June 11, 2027. FedRAMP 20x
  • The older FIPS standard has sunset.FIPS 140-2 validations moved to the historical list on September 21, 2026. New federal work needs 140-3, which Deepgram has for self-hosted. NIST
  • Bills of materials are now asked for agency by agency.The government-wide attestation rule was rescinded in January 2026, and the minimum contents were updated in July. CISA, analysis
