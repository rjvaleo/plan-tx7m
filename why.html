Why me.

I have never held the title Product Manager. I have been the product owner and builder of tools that security-conscious organizations work in, and I have been the buyer who could not get a tool approved.

What the posting asks, and what I have done

The posting asks forWhat I have done
Enterprise product for security-conscious customersI co-built the requirements app that ten vendor teams work in on a tolling modernization program. I built its role-based access: groups, inherited roles, a preview of every access change before it is saved, and refusals that say why. It fails closed without a key. Case study
Understanding procurement psychologyI have been the blocked buyer. On that program any add-on needed a security review, and I had no admin rights. So I built the planning board myself, as a single file that runs offline on locked-down networks. Case study
Compliance experienceSOX change control twice, at a publicly traded retailer and at a bank. I set segregation-of-duties controls so nobody could both submit and approve a production change. The retailer’s go-live landed on time.

An AI-native working styleThis site. Also four music products shipped with AI agents behind more than 9,000 automated tests, and a loop where local models work coding jobs against hidden tests. I check the agents’ numbers: a reported 682 tests turned out to be 384. How I work
Autonomy, with imperfect requirementsI run the release train for those ten vendor teams. Nobody asked for the planning board or the interviews with every lead before planning. Both are now how the program plans.

The gap, plainly

I have not shipped FedRAMP, FIPS or single sign-on.

I have shipped access control and audit-ready change control, and I know what it is to wait on a vendor’s security answers. I learn a new domain by reading everything and then sitting with the people who run it. This site is the first half of that, done in a day.

I also know voice. At Meta I produced the recordings that trained its AI voices, and supported the voice in the Ray-Ban Stories glasses through launch.
